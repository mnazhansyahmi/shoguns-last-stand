# Credits

Shogun's Last Stand draws all of its art and generates its placeholder sound effects in code. The music and
the sound effects below are third-party assets, used under licences that allow commercial use. The rule
(CLAUDE.md): a third-party asset is allowed only if its licence permits commercial use and it is credited here.
This file is also shown on the Credits screen of the main menu.

## Music

**Free Japanese Fantasy Music Pack** by Momiziba

Free Music : MOMIZizm MUSiC - https://music.storyinvention.com/en/

Licence: free to use, also in commercial games, as long as the credit line above is shown (the credit is REQUIRED
and must stay in the game and on the Credits screen). Terms and the full pack: https://music.storyinvention.com/en/

Tracks used (looping versions, in `assets/audio/music/`):

- **Voice of Evening Calm** (`Voice of Evening Calm.mp3`): the main menu and the upgrade shop.
- **Honobono Teahouse** (`Honobono Teahouse_Loop.ogg`): the stage select screen.
- **Mountain God's Shrine** (`Mountain God's Shrine_Loop.ogg`): battles.
- **Shamisen Samurai Rock** (`Shamisen Samurai Rock_Loop.ogg`): the boss fight (The Fallen Daimyo).

## Sound effects

**Impact Sounds**, **Interface Sounds** and **RPG Audio** by Kenney (https://www.kenney.nl)

Licence: Creative Commons Zero (CC0), https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/. No credit is required;
it is given here anyway. The licence texts are in `assets/audio/sfx_kenney/licenses/`.

Files used (`assets/audio/sfx_kenney/`), and where:

- Sword swings: `knifeSlice`, `knifeSlice2`, `drawKnife1`, `drawKnife2`, `drawKnife3` (RPG Audio).
- Sword hits: `impactMetal_light_000` to `impactMetal_light_003` (Impact Sounds).
- Heavy hits: `impactMetal_heavy_000` to `impactMetal_heavy_002` (Impact Sounds).
- Enemies falling: `impactSoft_heavy_000` to `impactSoft_heavy_002` (Impact Sounds).
- The village gate being hit: `impactWood_heavy_000` to `impactWood_heavy_002` (Impact Sounds).
- Button clicks: `click_001` to `click_003` (Interface Sounds).
- Summoning an ally: `impactBell_heavy_000`; the Ultimate becoming ready: `impactBell_heavy_002` (Impact Sounds).

## Made for the game

All other sound effects (the dash, the spirit slash, healing, magic, the victory and defeat stings and the
fallbacks for everything above) are generated by `tools/generate_sfx.gd`. All characters, backdrops and
effects are drawn in code. The UI uses the fonts installed on the player's system.
