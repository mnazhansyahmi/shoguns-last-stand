# Shogun's Last Stand

**Free public playtest · v0.2.0 · Windows x64**

Hold the village gate against the undead. Fight with your katana, summon an army
and spend your rewards on upgrades between battles.

## In this playtest

- Ten campaign stages across **Bamboo Outskirts** and the new **Burning Village**.
- Face **The Fallen Daimyo** and **The Burning General**, with painted boss bars,
  dramatic entrances and reworked sword attacks.
- New enemies: **Spectral Archer**, **Shield Bearer** and the hovering **Flying Spirit**.
  Read enemy briefing cards before battle, or revisit them from pause.
- A three-hit katana combo, heavy strike, dash, Spirit Slash and a charged Ultimate.
- Unlock the Bow and War Cry as you progress.
- Six summonable allies: Ashigaru, Yumi Archer, Shrine Healer, Spear Guardian,
  Onmyoji and Hatamoto.
- Command allies to charge or hold. Buy upgrades with earned gold and keep your progress.
- Higher upgrade tiers, **Gate Armour** and **Fire Resistance**. Purchase caps open
  as you clear stages; previously owned upgrades are kept.
- Painted worlds, gates and portals for both regions: misty bamboo at sunset and
  a burning village with drifting smoke, embers and animated fires.
- A living painted main menu, clearer settings tabs and a more dramatic Ultimate.

This is an early public playtest. I want to hear how the combat feels, which
stages are too easy or frustrating, and what needs explaining more clearly.

## Download and play

Download the Windows ZIP, extract the entire folder, then open
**ShogunsLastStand.exe**. Keep the `.pck` file beside it. You do not need Godot.

**Windows x64 only.** A graphics driver supporting OpenGL 3.3 is required.
No browser or mobile build is included in this release.

**Updating from v0.1?** Extract v0.2.0 into a new folder. Your public-playtest save
stays in `%APPDATA%\ShogunsLastStand`; keep it to retain your progress. Clear stage 5
to enter Burning Village. Do not use Reset Progress unless you want to start over.

## Keyboard controls

| Action | Key |
| --- | --- |
| Move | A / D or arrow keys |
| Attack / heavy / dash | J / K / L |
| Spirit Slash / Ultimate | U / I |
| Bow / War Cry | E / Q (unlocked through progression) |
| Allies charge / hold | F |
| Summon | 1–6 or click an unlocked portrait |
| Pause / back | Esc |

The game includes controller mappings and on-screen touch controls. Open
**Settings → Gameplay → Controls** for the complete layout. Physical-controller hardware
testing is still pending. Settings also offers reduced decorative motion and
toggles for blood and screen shake.

## Tell me what you think

Please leave a comment with:

1. Which stage you reached.
2. What felt good about the combat.
3. What felt confusing, unfair or repetitive.
4. Any bug and the steps needed to reproduce it.

Screenshots or short clips are especially useful. Include the build version
**0.2.0** when reporting a problem.

## Known playtest limitations

- Balance, walking animation and weapon poses are still being refined; stage 10
  is intended to be played with upgrades.
- Busy fights and fire effects can cause frame-time spikes. The original strict
  8 ms performance target is not consistently met.
- Testing has covered the development Windows machine; wider PC compatibility
  and physical-controller testing remain pending.
