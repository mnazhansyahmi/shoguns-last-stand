# Approved slim swordsman direction

## Scope and authority

This is a precisely specified character replacement inside an established Godot
game. The user supplied a slim, flowing ink swordsman reference, selected the
original concept, then approved implementation on 2026-10-21. See
`approved_concept.png` and `../../PRODUCT.md`. No FORM roll or historical seed was
run for this scoped replacement; no seed is claimed.

## Visual contract

- **Identity:** lean athletic silhouette, bare head and long tied ponytail, light
  black kimono and gathered hakama, wrapped ankles, sandals, grey sash, muted red
  waist tails. No helmet, shoulder plates or bulky armour.
- **Material:** original ink-wash raster layers with weathered cloth edges and
  visible folds. Keep transparent negative space and connected joints. Source
  prompts are embedded in the production PNGs and recorded in their provenance.
- **Composition:** retain the existing sunset landscape, moon, menu hierarchy and
  battle HUD. The static menu portrait stands on the outcrop with both feet in
  contact. Gameplay retains the animated bone rig and readable equipment.
- **Behaviour:** keep movement/combat statistics, animation timing, fixed-length
  IK and planted feet. Hair sway and cloth follow the visual motion; shared
  textures remain cached. Preserve the Bow and War Cry equipment transitions.
- **Quality bar:** the approved concept governs anatomy and material finish;
  existing game captures govern screen layout. No exposed geometric cloth filler,
  detached neck, opaque background, floating sole or missing skill equipment.

## Evidence

Review menu at 1280x720 and the wide host viewport, battle skill states, close-up
idle/raised blade/lunge, pose sheet, sampled run strip and icon sheet. Static
captures establish appearance, not a human judgement of animation smoothness.
Tests and the renderer benchmark supply separate mechanical evidence.
