# Samurai consistency pass — approved scope

This is an explicit three-part refinement on `main`, not a new visual world.
The user's latest request supersedes the rigged title character. No FORM roll or
composition reproduction is claimed. The existing sunset landscape and menus
remain the native visual context.

## Identity

Keep the same bareheaded, slim and fast young swordsman: tied black ponytail,
charcoal kimono, ivory collar, grey sash, restrained red cloth, gathered hakama,
wrapped calves and sandals. Match the existing painted face, without armour.

## Material

Battle body parts must share the face's fine painted ink detail and coherent
sampling. Preserve the existing logical layer roles, fixed bone lengths and
gripping hands. Use original generated raster painting, with exact prompts and
tool/date records in ASSET_LICENSES.md. Source registration may change; mechanics
may not. Filtering must not make the body soft beside an unminified face.

## Composition

The title is ONE full-body illustration standing naturally on the native rock,
moon behind the head. Right hand holds the katana low around the middle of the
hilt; the other hand is relaxed. No limb rig on the title. The HUD uses a painted
three-quarter head-and-shoulders bust designed for its small circular frame.
Keep navigation, Ultimate ring, READY plate and HUD layout.

## Behaviour

Battle keeps the existing IK/gait, ground contact, combat and hand-over-hilt
ordering. Title motion is confined to free hair, sash, hem and a sword glint;
face, hands, body and soles are pinned. Reduced decorative motion remains 15%.
The HUD uses a cached painted texture, not a live rig or SubViewport.

## Quality bar

The approved identity reference is `assets/art/roster/samurai_head.png` and the
user's supplied slim ink swordsman reference. Judge anatomy, connected paint,
matching detail at battle size, readable eyes at roughly 120px, planted soles,
clear hilt grip and the absence of puppet seams in the title. Existing game
captures govern the landscape and navigation. Native Godot has no DOM/CSS
detector. Renderer frames establish appearance and sampled pose changes; clips
are supplied for user playback and do not establish a human smoothness verdict.
